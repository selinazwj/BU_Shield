"What do I do if ICE comes to my dorm door?"
— Anon Terrier
Do NOT open the door. Ask them to slide the warrant under the door. Call the BU legal line immediately. You do not have to let anyone in without a judicial warrant signed by a federal judge. — Peer Advisor
"If my SEVIS record is terminated, what happens to my enrollment?"
— Anon Terrier
Contact ISSO immediately at bu.edu/isso — they have immigration lawyers on call. Federal courts have issued protections for some BU students. Do not leave campus without speaking to ISSO first. — ISSO Advisor
"I witnessed ICE near GSU. Who should I tell?"

Use the Map tab to post an anonymous report. You can also call BUPD at 617-353-2121. The more reports we have, the faster the community stays safe. — BU Shield Team

Know the Difference
✕ Admin Warrant
Form I-200 or I-205. Signed by ICE only. Does NOT allow entry into dorms or private spaces.
✓ Judicial Warrant
Signed by a federal judge. Required to enter dorms, apartments, and private areas.
Massachusetts AG (2026): ICE needs a judicial warrant to enter dorms.
What to do
✓
Do NOT open the door without a judicial warrant.
✓
Do NOT sign anything without a lawyer.
✓
Ask for the officer's name and badge number.
✓
Record the interaction if safe to do so.
✓
You have the right to remain silent.
