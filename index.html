Call Your Representatives

Use your voice. These scripts are ready to use — calling takes less than 3 minutes and is more effective than an email.

Script 1 — ICE Accountability
"The country has watched fatal encounters involving federal immigration agents, and there is widespread concern about aggressive tactics. I'm alarmed that state and local leaders and civil-rights groups have filed lawsuits alleging unconstitutional stops and arrests. Please vote NO on more ICE funding and support amendments that require warrants, ban suspicionless stops, and create real accountability. Will you commit to that?"
Source: sharonmcmahon.com/act
Script 2 — Protect University Students
"I'm a constituent calling about immigration enforcement at universities. Students at schools like Boston University are being detained without judicial warrants based on protected speech. I urge you to support legislation requiring judicial warrants for campus enforcement and protecting students' First Amendment rights. Will you commit to that?"

Know the Difference
✕ Admin Warrant
Form I-200 or I-205. Signed by ICE only. Does NOT allow entry into dorms.
✓ Judicial Warrant
Signed by a federal judge. Required to enter dorms and private areas.
Massachusetts AG (2026): ICE needs a judicial warrant to enter dorms.
What to do
✓
Do NOT open the door without a judicial warrant.
✓
Do NOT sign anything without a lawyer.
✓
Ask for the officer's name and badge number.
✓
Record the interaction if safe to do so.
✓
You have the right to remain silent.
