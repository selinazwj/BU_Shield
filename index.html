Know the Difference
❌ Admin Warrant
Form I-200 or I-205. Signed by ICE only. Does NOT allow entry into your dorm or private space.
✅ Judicial Warrant
Signed by a federal judge. Required to enter dorms, apartments, and private areas.
Massachusetts AG: ICE needs a judicial warrant to enter dorms. (2026)
What to do
✓
Do NOT open the door without a judicial warrant.
✓
Do NOT sign anything without a lawyer present.
✓
Stay calm. Ask for the officer's name and badge number.
✓
Record the interaction if it is safe to do so.
✓
You have the right to remain silent. You do not have to answer questions.
